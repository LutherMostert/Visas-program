## What changes the scenario

Merits are not the variable. The three levers below are:

- **Cession perfection** — governs our ability to realise value against the N$100m vessel without fighting unsecured-creditor priority. If perfected, upside/base become materially more likely. If not, downside probability rises.
- **Vessel location** — in Walvis Bay = arrestable = enforceable award. Moved = enforcement becomes a cross-border problem and timing extends materially.
- **Mabasen solvency timing** — if they fold before enforcement, we compete with preferent claims against the vessel sale proceeds. The N$100m cushion protects us in all but a pile-on scenario, but timing matters.
